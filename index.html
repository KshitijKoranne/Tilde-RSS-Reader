Tilde — a free RSS reader for Mac and the web

Tilde collects new posts from the blogs, news sites and newsletters you pick, and nothing else. It is free, there is no account, and everything stays on your own device.

You will reach the end. No endless scroll, no algorithm deciding what you see, and nothing recommended to you.

Open Tilde in your browser · Download on the Mac App Store

Why people use it

You actually finish

Tilde shows what is new since you last looked, and once you have read it the list is empty and says so. Nothing refills it to keep you busy.

You choose what shows up

Paste a link to any site, or bring your whole list over from another reader in one OPML file. Tilde never reorders your feeds or slips in posts you did not ask for.

You can find it again

Every article you open is saved on your own computer, in full. Search a half-remembered phrase months later and it is still there, even if the site that published it is gone.

In a browser, or on your Mac

The web version needs nothing installed and works in any browser. The Mac app lives in the Dock, fetches every source directly, and reads with the network off. Both are free.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • j / k: next and previous article
  • o: open it
  • m: mark read or unread
  • s: save for later
  • /: search everything you have read
  • a: add a site
