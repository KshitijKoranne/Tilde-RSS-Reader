Changelog

What changed in each version, newest first.

1.3.0

4 October 2026

Four small options, all in Settings, all off until you turn them on.

  • Undo for Mark all read. A button shows for a few seconds so you can take it back.
  • Every shortcut in the key strip. Adds a (add a source) and r (refresh) to the row under the list.
  • Refresh when you return. Tilde checks your sources when you come back after a few minutes away.
  • Dock badge. Your unread count on the Tilde icon in the Dock. Mac app only.

If you change nothing, Tilde works exactly as before. Your feeds, saved articles and read state are untouched.

1.2.0

29 August 2026

  • Groups. Give a source a group in Settings and it folds up under that name in the sidebar, with its own unread count.
  • Search that stays quick. Tilde searches an index of your archive instead of reading every article, so it is as fast in year three as in week one.
  • Keep articles for. Choose to let go of read articles older than three months, six months or a year. Saved articles always stay.
  • OPML keeps your folders. Folders come in as groups and go out as folders again.

1.1.0

28 August 2026

  • Read the full article. Some feeds publish only a link, Hacker News for one. One button fetches the page and shows the article inside Tilde. Only when you ask.

1.0.0

28 August 2026

The first release: a free RSS reader for the web and the Mac. No account, no ads, nothing recommended to you.
